## Built

`src/components/sections/mission-journey.tsx` — a server component, directly
after the hero. A 500vh section with a stage pinned to the viewport; five
chapters stacked in it. One named view timeline (`--cinema`) on the section
drives everything:

- each chapter fades in and out over its window, windows overlapping by 8% so
  frames dissolve into each other;
- each image pushes in slowly (scale 1.14 → 1) across its window;
- each caption rises in, holds, and lifts out;
- a progress bar fills along the bottom.

Captions are real `<h3>` and `<p>`. Images are `alt=""` — atmosphere, not
evidence; the text carries the meaning. Every sentence is from confirmed facts:
the source types (and that a report uses only what it needs), SQL/Python,
business rules, Excel output, region-wise summaries, pan-India email, WhatsApp
and SMS APIs in TruBot. **No numbers.**

**Midnight only.** The imagery is navy and cyan; on the other themes it would
break theme isolation, so the section is `display: none` there — and a lazy
image in a hidden subtree is never fetched. Asserted in E2E: 0 requests.

**Fallbacks** — the default CSS is five stacked chapters (image with caption),
used when `animation-timeline` is unsupported, below 768px, and under reduced
motion.

## Revision (same day): landing frame, sharper chapters, stage strip

Arvind reported the chapter images as blurred, and supplied a zip of the
storyboard plus two new single images.

**Root cause of the blur.** The chapters were full-bleed with a 14% push-in.
The sources are 1024–1505px wide, so on a 1440px screen that was a 1.3–1.6×
upscale, and far worse on a phone where `cover` crops a wide image to a tall
screen. **The zip's 15 panels were not used** — each is a ~384×289 crop of one
1536×1024 composite, upscaled to 3840px. That is ten times enlargement, and no
format or compression recovers detail that was never there.

**Changes**

- **Landing frame** (`landing-scene.tsx`), before the hero, Midnight only: the
  1672×941 "data flows into the bot" image, full screen, as a CSS background
  (so other themes never request it). Words rise in on load; on scroll they
  lift away, the picture eases in by at most 6%, and fades as the hero
  arrives. Timeline is `scroll(root)` — a view timeline measured 20% into its
  range at scroll 0 for a subject taller than the viewport. Portrait screens
  put the words on top and the bot hub below.
- **Stage strip** on the landing (≥1024×700): five crops from the second
  storyboard — **picture area only, baked titles cropped away** — ~300×284px,
  shown at or below native size. Staggered rise on load; hover lifts the card
  and scales the image from 0.94 up to 1, never past it; on scroll the strip
  drops away. Labels are real text, confirmed facts only (TruBot; SFTP/FTP/S3/
  database; SQL/Python/Excel & VBA; WhatsApp, SMS, ID creation & deactivation
  APIs; MIS reports, email).
- **Mission chapters** now sit in a framed card (≤880px, caption beside it);
  the settle-in scales up *to* 1, never past. Chapter 1 uses the new "robot at
  the pipeline" image. All re-encoded at higher quality.
- Not used: the "Building Intelligent Automation" mockups — nav, buttons and
  headline are baked into the picture and would duplicate the real ones.

**Images:** landing 183 KB, strip 91 KB, chapters 1.0 MB large / 223 KB small.
All Midnight-only; 0 requests on other themes (asserted). Shared JS still
102 kB, home 156 kB.

**Tests:** 465 unit/component. Full E2E: 497 passed, 8 failed — two stale
assertions in the new spec (stacked chapters are now in flow, not `relative`;
the landing's top read during the page's entrance transition) fixed, and one
theme-motion check that passed alone. Re-run of the mission/landing, flicker,
accessibility and theme-motion specs on all four viewports: **96 passed**.
Overflow 0 at 1280, 1440, 1920 and 390.
